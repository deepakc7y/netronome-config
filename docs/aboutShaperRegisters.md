Description: Details about Shaper-related NFP Control and Status Rgisters (CSRs).

The register below enables or disables the Shaper.
```xpb:Nbi0IsldXpbMap.NbiTopXpbMap.TrafficManager.TrafficManagerReg.TrafficManagerConfig.ShaperEnable=0x0```

The remainder of the registers related to shapers can be found here:
```xpb:Nbi0IsldXpbMap.NbiTopXpbMap.TrafficManager.TMShaperReg.Shaper```

They can be classified as:

- ShaperMaxOvershoot0-144
    - Rate limiting shaper maximum overshoot register.
    - Configures the maximum allowed overshoot for the leaky bucket. 
    - The level 2 shaper registers are at offsets 0-127, the level 1 shaper registers are at offsets 128-143, and the level 0 shaper register is at offset 144

- ShaperRate0-144
    - Shaper rate register. 
    - Configures the maximum allowed rate. 
    - The level 2 shaper registers are at offsets 0-127, the level 1 shaper registers are at offsets 128-143, and the level 0 shaper register is at offset 144.

- ShaperRateAdjust0-144
    - Shaper rate adjust register. 
    - Configures the fixed data rate adjustment for packet overhead. 
    - The level 2 shaper registers are at offsets 0-127, the level 1 shaper registers are at offsets 128-143, and the level 0 shaper register is at offset 144.

- ShaperStatus0-144
    - Shaper status register. 
    - Contains read-only status information for the rate liming shaper. 
    - The level 2 shaper registers are at offsets 0-127, the level 1 shaper registers are at offsets 128-143, and the level 0 shaper register is at offset 144.

- ShaperThreshold0-144
    - Rate limiting shaper threshold register. 
    - Configures the desired threshold level of the leaky bucket. 
    - The level 2 shaper registers are at offsets 0-127, the level 1 shaper registers are at offsets 128-143, and the level 0 shaper register is at offset 144.
